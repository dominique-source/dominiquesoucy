01 — THE MAIN PORTRAIT
========================================

Current problem:

The existing hero portrait shows too much torso and the ball Dominique is holding.

This produces an overly corporate and sports-promotional impression.

Required correction:

- Increase the apparent face size approximately 50–70%.
- Remove the ball entirely through cropping.
- Focus on Dominique's eyes, smile, nose, beard and chin.
- Keep the portrait positioned center-right.
- Allow the forehead and shoulders to extend beyond the visual frame.
- Keep the photographic subject recognizable.
- Convert to refined cinematic black and white.
- Add subtle contrast and grain without reconstructing facial detail.

The face should dominate the hero collage.

Visitors should feel as though they are encountering a real person, rather than viewing a corporate portrait.

Use object-position, CSS masking and responsive cropping where appropriate.

Do not upscale a low-resolution source beyond acceptable quality.

If the available photo cannot support the required crop without significant pixelation, use the best authentic source and document the limitation.
